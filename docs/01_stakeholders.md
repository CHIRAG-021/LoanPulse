# Stakeholders

## LoanPulse: LoanMaster Loan Management

Based on the selected **Option 2 — Loan Application & Repayment Tracking** project description.

| Role | What they do | Pain today | How they benefit | Influence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customer / Borrower | Registers, submits loan applications, views application status, loan details and repayment history | May have difficulty tracking application and repayment information across different records or channels | Gets one place to apply for a loan and track status, loan details and repayments | High |
| Loan Officer | Reviews applications, checks submitted information, performs predefined rule-based risk assessment, and recommends approval/rejection | Manual review and scattered application information can make processing harder to track | Gets a centralized application workflow and consistent rule-based assessment | High |
| Loan Manager / Decision-Maker | Reviews loan applications and makes the final approve/reject decision within the system | Difficult to get a clear view of application status and supporting information when handled manually | Gets a centralized view of applications, risk results, and processing status | High |
| Repayment / Finance Staff | Records disbursement and repayment information and keeps loan payment records updated | Maintaining repayment records manually can lead to tracking difficulties | Gets organized loan and repayment records in one system | Medium |
| System Administrator | Manages users, roles, system configuration, and basic operational data | User and access management can be difficult without centralized controls | Can manage access and maintain the application efficiently | Medium |
| Project Owner / College Evaluator | Reviews the project to assess whether the proposed workflow and implementation meet project objectives | Needs a clear, demonstrable system with understandable scope and requirements | Gets a complete student-level demonstration of a loan-management workflow | Medium |

## Recommended stakeholders

The recommended **5 stakeholders** for the project scope are:

1. **Customer / Borrower** — primary user who applies for and tracks loans.
2. **Loan Officer** — operational user who reviews applications and performs the predefined risk assessment.
3. **Loan Manager / Decision-Maker** — decision-maker responsible for approving or rejecting applications.
4. **Repayment / Finance Staff** — handles disbursement and repayment records.
5. **System Administrator** — manages users, roles, and system configuration.

This selection keeps the project manageable while covering the main loan lifecycle from application through approval, disbursement, and repayment.

> **Verification note:** The exact responsibilities and job titles used in a real lending organization should be verified against the organization being modeled. LoanPulse is a student project and does not represent a real financial institution's organizational structure.
