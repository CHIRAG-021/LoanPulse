Core Platform Capabilities

Engineered strictly around the verified, rule-based LoanPulse lending operations workflow.

Application Tracking

Customers submit structured loan requests with verified income, principal amount, term length, and loan purpose with real-time status visibility.

Deterministic Assessment

Deterministic calculations evaluate eligibility against income thresholds and loan-to-income caps with full transparency and no opaque black boxes.

Role-Based Workflow

Rigorous separation of duties ensures Customers apply, Officers evaluate ratios, and Managers hold final decision and disbursement authority.

Loan Product Exploration

Borrowers can explore active loan products with published annual interest rates, minimum and maximum amounts, and defined tenure boundaries.

Document & KYC Submission

Upload required identity and income proofs with clear status tracking (VERIFIED, PENDING, REJECTED) and seamless resubmission support.

Transparent Auditing

Every review step, assessment ratio, officer note, and manager decision is recorded in audit logs with full relational integrity and timestamps.

The LoanPulse Operational Lifecycle

Traceable from initial borrower application to final disbursement and customer notification.

1

Explore & Apply

Select an active loan product, specify amount and tenure, and submit employment details.

2

Document Submission

Upload required identity and income documents with clear review states.

3

Officer Assessment

Deterministic credit rules evaluate Debt-to-Income and lending policy thresholds.

4

Manager Decision

Executive review approves or rejects the application with complete audit logging.

5

Disbursement & Alerts

Approved loans progress to recorded disbursement with verified release dates and in-app updates.

Built on Verified Security & Governance

Engineered with rigorous architectural controls to protect your data and prevent unauthorized operations.

Tab-Scoped Session Isolation

Every browser tab maintains its own isolated authentication context, preventing session bleeding across roles and active windows.

CSRF & Request Security

All state-modifying requests are protected with cryptographic tokens to safeguard against cross-site request forgery attacks.

Enforced Role Boundaries

Strict server-side decorators enforce RBAC policies: Customers never access staff actions, and Officers cannot execute manager decisions.

Full Audit Trail Integrity

Every review note, ratio assessment, and status change is persisted in append-only audit records with timestamps and actor tracking.
