Core Platform Capabilities

Engineered strictly around the documented LoanPulse operational workflow.

Application Management

Customers submit structured loan requests with validated income, principal amount, term length, and loan purpose.

Rule-Based Assessment

Deterministic calculations evaluate eligibility against income thresholds and loan-to-income caps with no opaque AI black boxes.

Role-Based Workflow

Rigorous separation of duties ensures Customers apply, Officers assess, and Managers hold final decision authority.

Disbursement Tracking

Approved loans transition cleanly through disbursement recording with verified amounts and execution dates.

Lifecycle Notifications

Automated status notifications alert stakeholders whenever an application moves between workflow states.

Transparent Auditing

Every review, assessment ratio, and executive decision is stored with full relational integrity and timestamps.

The LoanPulse Operational Lifecycle

Traceable from initial borrower application to final disbursement and customer notification.

1

Customer Submits

Application saved in SQLite with PENDING_REVIEW state.

2

Officer Assesses

Deterministic rules calculate loan-to-income ratios and eligibility.

3

Manager Decides

Executive review approves or rejects the application.

4

Loan Disburses

Approved loans are recorded as DISBURSED with release date.

5

In-App Alerts

Real-time lifecycle update generated for the borrower.
